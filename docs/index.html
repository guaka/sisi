sisi

A personal Nos.social fork for following people across Nostr, Mastodon, and Bluesky — with curated follow packs and a daily stop after about half an hour.

What it does

  • Easy follows — paste Mastodon or Bluesky profile URLs / handles; Mostr (+ Bridgy Fed for Bluesky) maps them to Nostr.
  • Follow packs — one-tap follow for journalists, old-school media, and other curated sets (NIP-51 starter packs where available).
  • Time nudge — soft reminder around 25 minutes; blocks the UI after 30, with two short snoozes.

Follow packs

Same catalog ships in the app (packs.json). Bluesky accounts only resolve if they opted into Bridgy Fed.
