Nos client

sisi

A Nos.social fork for following people across Nostr, Mastodon, and Bluesky — with curated follow packs and a daily stop after about half an hour.

How follow packs work

Packs are used inside the sisi iOS app, not from this webpage. This site is a public catalog of the same lists that ship in the app.

  1. Open sisi on your phone.
  2. Go to the Discover tab.
  3. Tap a pack (for example “Old-school media” or “Stringer journalists”).
  4. Review who resolved, then tap Follow all.

Accounts are followed on Nostr. Mastodon and Bluesky profiles are bridged via Mostr (and Bridgy Fed for Bluesky). If someone hasn’t enabled the bridge, they simply won’t resolve.

What else it does

  • Easy follows — paste a Mastodon or Bluesky profile URL or handle in Discover search.
  • Time nudge — reminder around 25 minutes; the app locks after about 30, with two short snoozes.
